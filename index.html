Privacy Policy

Rome Warfare

Effective Date: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Rome Warfare ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

Summary: Rome Warfare is a free game supported by advertising. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and usage data to display ads. Where required (EEA, UK), we ask for your consent first. Leaderboards and game achievements use Google Play Games Services. We do not sell your data.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information Collected Automatically

When you use Google Play Games Services features (such as leaderboards and achievements), the following information may be collected:

  • Google Play Games Player ID: A unique identifier assigned by Google Play Games Services
  • Game Activity: Your battle stats, campaign progress, and high scores submitted to leaderboards
  • Display Name: Your Google Play Games profile name (as configured in your Google account)

1.2 Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

The app displays banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob unless you purchase the optional ad-removal product. Google and its partners may collect and process the following through the AdMob SDK:

  • Advertising ID (resettable by you in your device settings)
  • IP address, device and OS information, app version
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) and approximate location derived from the IP address

This data is used to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and, if you consent, to personalise ads. See how Google uses data when you use partner apps.

1.3 Purchase Information

If you buy the optional ad-removal product or in-game items, Google Play processes the transaction. We do not receive or store your payment card or other financial details. The app stores your ad-free entitlement locally and checks your existing purchases through Google Play to restore them.

1.4 Information Stored Locally

The following data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to external servers:

  • Gameplay statistics (battles won/lost, troops deployed, campaign progress)
  • In-game currency, upgrades, and army unlocks
  • Local achievement progress
  • App preferences, audio settings, and gameplay configurations
  • Your ad-free entitlement status

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for:

  • Leaderboard & Achievement Functionality: Displaying your military rankings and unlocking achievements within Google Play Games
  • Game Features: Managing local game saves, battle progress, and army upgrades
  • Advertising: Showing ads through Google AdMob to support the free application
  • Purchase Restoration: Restoring the optional ad-removal or in-app purchases through Google Play

We do NOT use your information for:

  • Selling your personal information
  • Our own external user profiling or data mining

3. Data Sharing

Your data is shared only in the following circumstances:

  • Public Leaderboards: Your display name and battle scores are visible to other players on Google Play Games leaderboards
  • Google Play Games Services: Data is processed by Google according to their Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Advertising data is processed by Google and its ad partners

We do not sell your personal information or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Security

We take data security seriously:

  • All data transmitted to Google Play Games Services is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Local data is stored securely in Android's app storage sandbox
  • We do not store your game data or personal details on external servers owned by us

5. Your Choices and Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Opt-out of Leaderboards: You can play Rome Warfare without signing into Google Play Games. All offline campaign features will work normally.
  • Ad Consent and Personalisation: In the EEA/UK, you can change your ad consent at any time under Settings → "Ad Privacy Settings". On Android, you can also reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation via Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Remove Ads: You can purchase the optional ad-removal product. Uninstalling the app does not cancel purchases; Google Play restores eligible purchases automatically.
  • Delete Local Data: Uninstall the app or clear app data in Android Settings to wipe all locally saved army progress and local data.
  • Delete Google Play Games Data: Visit Google Play Settings to manage or delete your saved Play Games data.
  • Manage Google Account: Control your global data options through your Google Account Privacy Settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Rome Warfare is a general audience game intended for a wide audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app contains animated tactical warfare and military themes; parental guidance is recommended.

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly serve targeted ads to them. Google Play Games Services has its own age restrictions and parental controls as described in Google's Family Policy.

7. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Games Services: For leaderboards and achievements. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): For advertising. AdMob policies and data use
  • Google Play Billing: For handling in-app purchases. Payments and purchase records are handled directly by Google Play; we do not process your financial details.

We do not use any additional third-party analytics SDKs or external tracking services.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the updated Privacy Policy on this page and revising the "Effective Date" at the top.

9. Data Retention

  • Local Data: Retained until you uninstall the app or clear app cache/data
  • Google Play Games Data: Retained according to Google's data retention policies until managed or deleted by you
  • Advertising and Purchase Data: Retained by Google and its partners according to their applicable privacy policies
